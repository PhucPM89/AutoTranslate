# Cover image sources

These images are stored locally and used as fallback covers when an uploaded book has no cover.
Each one ships in two sizes: `<name>.webp` (480x720, catalog cards) and `<name>-hero.webp`
(960x1440 or larger, the featured backdrop). Both are re-encoded from the originals below.

- `night-temple.webp`: [Illuminated Ancient Chinese Architecture at Night](https://www.pexels.com/photo/illuminated-ancient-chinese-architecture-at-night-29712655/) by Xiaodong Zhang on Pexels.
- `misty-pagoda.webp`: [Misty mountains with a pagoda on a peak](https://unsplash.com/photos/misty-mountains-with-a-pagoda-on-a-peak-uthlxCqIdyw) by Lianhao Qu on Unsplash.
- `lantern-temple.webp`: [Chinese Temple at Night](https://www.pexels.com/photo/chinese-temple-at-night-14614826/) by fei wang on Pexels.
