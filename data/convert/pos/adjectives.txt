# Attributive adjectives (形容词) — one per line.
#
# The grammar layer uses this for two rules:
#   1. ADJ + NOUN  -> NOUN + ADJ   (Vietnamese postposes the modifier)
#   2. ADJ + 的 + NOUN -> NOUN + ADJ (drop 的, then rule 1 applies)
#
# ONLY list adjectives that are attributive in practice. Words that are commonly
# adverbial (缓缓, 轻轻, 深深, 慢慢, 静静, 默默, 悄悄, 突然, 快速) must NOT be here:
# postposing them across a verb ("nhanh chóng di chuyển" -> "di chuyển nhanh
# chóng") is sometimes fine but often wrong, and adverbs are the common case.

# Totality quantifiers. They sit where an adjective sits (全部内情, 所有弟子), so
# the 的 rule must treat them as part of the head rather than as the head itself:
# 这件事的全部内情 is "toàn bộ nội tình của chuyện này", not "toàn bộ của chuyện
# này nội tình". Vietnamese keeps them in front, and the postposing rule only
# moves adjectives that precede a noun — which these do — so they are listed
# here and excluded from the swap by name.
