# Negative corpus — ordinary prose that the proper-noun layer must NOT touch, and
# constructions the grammar rules must leave alone.
#
# Every line contains a surname character, a place suffix, a classifier or a 的
# in a position where firing a rule would damage the sentence. Run:
#   node scripts/convert-eval.js --in data/convert/samples/negatives.txt
# and read the output: a Title-Cased word here is a false positive.
